How do I know which supplement category to choose?

Start with your goal—protein intake, muscle and weight goals, training performance or everyday wellness. Then send us your routine and preference on WhatsApp for a practical shortlist.

Can I confirm the flavour and pack size first?

Yes. Please share the product name, preferred flavour and size. We will confirm what is currently available before you decide.

Do you show prices on the website?

Stock and variants can change, so the store confirms the current product details directly before an order is placed.

Which areas can enquire for delivery?

We take enquiries from Kolar, Srinivaspur, Mulbagal, Bangarpet, Malur and nearby locations. Delivery options are confirmed using your address or pin code.

How do I contact I FIT FORMULA?

Call or WhatsApp +91 92060 14393. WhatsApp is best when you want to share a product name, flavour, pack size or delivery location.

Are supplements suitable for everyone?

Products should be chosen carefully based on your own needs. For medical conditions, pregnancy, medication use or other health concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional first.
